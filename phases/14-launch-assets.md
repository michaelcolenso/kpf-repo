# Phase 14: LAUNCH ASSETS

**Role**: Copywriter creating marketing materials
**Tools**: None — copywriting

## What You Do

Create every piece of marketing copy needed to sell and distribute the product. Use the language bank from `pain_map.json` and `knowledge_base.json` heavily — the buyer should feel like you're reading their mind.

### 1. Sales Page

Write a complete long-form sales page:

**Headline**: Problem + solution in one punchy line. Use language from the pain map.
- Formula: "[Desired Outcome] Without [Pain They Fear]"
- Example: "Get On Insurance Panels Without Spending $500 on a Credentialing Service"

**Problem Agitation**: 2-3 paragraphs that make the pain vivid. Use exact quotes from the language bank. The reader should think "this person gets it."

**Solution Presentation**: Introduce the product as the bridge from pain to outcome. Not features — transformation.

**What's Inside**: List every section and artifact with benefit-focused descriptions.
- Not: "Chapter 3: Application Process"
- But: "Chapter 3: The Exact Application Process (Step by Step, So You Never Wonder 'What's Next?')"

**Pricing with Anchoring**: Compare to alternatives.
- "Credentialing services charge $500-1500"
- "Online courses cost $199 but are generic"
- "This playbook: $97 — specific to your state, with every template included"

**Guarantee**: Simple risk reversal. "If this doesn't save you at least 10 hours, I'll refund you."

**CTA**: Clear, single action. "Get the Playbook → $97"

### 2. Gumroad Listing

Short, scannable version for the product page:
- 2-3 sentence description
- "What's inside" bullet list (5-7 items)
- "This is for you if..." (3-4 qualifying statements)
- "This is NOT for you if..." (1-2 disqualifying statements)

### 3. Lead Magnet

A free piece of content that demonstrates expertise and creates desire for the full product:
- Hook: One quick win from the product (e.g., "The 6 Documents You Need Before Applying to Any Panel")
- Content: Deliver the quick win completely — no teasing
- CTA: "Want the full playbook? [link]"

### 4. Launch Posts (3-5)

Write 3-5 social media posts for launch. Each should stand alone:

| Post | Type | Goal |
|------|------|------|
| 1 | Problem awareness | Describe the pain. No pitch. End with "Sound familiar?" |
| 2 | Solution reveal | "I built the thing I wish existed when..." |
| 3 | Social proof / case study | Use research data as proxy ("Therapists report spending 20-40 hours on this...") |
| 4 | Urgency or scarcity | "Launch pricing ends [date]" or "First 50 buyers get [bonus]" |
| 5 | Final call | Direct pitch with CTA |

Each post: 150-300 words. Written for the platforms where the target audience lives (from `product_brief.json` distribution channels).

### 5. FAQ

5-7 most likely questions, based on:
- Pain map research (what do people ask?)
- Competitor reviews (what do buyers complain about?)
- Format concerns ("Is this a course? Do I need to watch videos?")

### 6. Objection Handling

Address the top 3-4 objections:
- **Price**: "It's too expensive" → anchor against alternatives
- **Time**: "I don't have time for another course" → this isn't a course, it's a do-it-alongside-me system
- **Fit**: "This isn't for my situation" → be specific about who it IS for
- **Trust**: "Why should I listen to you?" → credibility signals
